## 风格锚点
Sun-baked Italian film still, Techniscope , extreme close-up on a sweating face or a vast empty landscape, hard single-source sun, deep clean black shadow, ochre and rust and olive, dust in the beam, long-lens compression, frame within a doorway, 35mm grain, patient, tactile, unsentimental.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a weathered face in extreme close-up, or a lone figure in a dry landscape; dust, leather, stone.
- 景别 / 构图：extreme close-up or extreme wide, no middle ground; off-axis, eye on the upper third, doorway framing.
- 镜头 / 焦段：25mm for landscape interiors, 135–250mm for faces; compressed perspective on the close-up.
- 光线：one hard sun or one practical, deep clean shadow, a small reflector only to keep life in the eye.
- 色彩：ochre, rust red, olive green, bone white, all from materials; skin with blood in it.
- 材质：sweat, dust, leather, rough wood, lime plaster, worn cloth.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动（只选一种）：very slow dolly in.
- 主体运动：almost none; the face holds, the eyes move, breath and dust do the rest.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：10–15 seconds, 24fps, natural motion blur, no slow motion.

## 负面提示
no even soft fill light, no designed symbolic colour scheme, no shallow background blur, no fast cutting, no cold white or orange skin, no modern LED white, no grey shadows, no slow-motion violence, no clean digital texture.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩 / 镜头 / 光线 / 构图 / 材质：ochre-rust-olive; 135–250mm close-ups; single hard sun; off-axis eye-line; sweat and dust.
- 禁止漂移：no teal-orange grade, no soft romantic glow, no saturated poster palette, no handheld, no modern interiors.
