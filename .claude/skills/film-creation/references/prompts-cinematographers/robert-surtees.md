## 风格锚点
Naturalistic deep-focus film still, wide frame, 28–40mm lens at eye level, foreground hands and distant figures equally sharp, flat daylight with bounce fill, restrained low-saturation colour, accurate skin, real dust and wood, figures placed at both edges with open space between, 35mm grain, observational, unforced, documentary calm.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：two or three people in a real room, street or landscape; foreground objects and distant action share the frame.
- 景别 / 构图：wide to medium wide, lateral staging, figures at both edges, centre left open for movement or event.
- 镜头 / 焦段：28–40mm wide-angle stopped down for deep focus; long lens only to bring distant figures forward.
- 光线：natural sun or window light with large bounce boards, ratio, no coloured side light.
- 色彩：low-saturation natural colour, accurate skin, ungraded sky and dust; no unifying tint.
- 材质：real wood, stone, dust, coarse fabric, worn paint, practical interiors.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动（只选一种）：slow tracking follow.
- 主体运动：people walk through the depth of the frame, crossing between foreground and background.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：10–18 seconds, 24fps, natural motion blur, no slow motion.

## 负面提示
no shallow depth of field, no coloured dramatic side light, no colour as subject, no centred advertising composition, no isolated close-up, no warm-orange skin grade, no handheld dialogue coverage, no stretched widescreen, no digital HDR.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩 / 镜头 / 光线 / 构图 / 材质：low-saturation natural palette; 28–40mm deep focus; single sun plus bounce; lateral staging; real dust and wood.
- 禁止漂移：no teal-orange LUT, no shallow bokeh, no studio fill, no modern glass architecture, no saturated primaries.
