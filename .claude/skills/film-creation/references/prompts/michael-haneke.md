## 风格锚点
Cinematic film still, surveillance composure, a middle-class interior held in one fixed wide frame, camera planted at the far corner of the room, 40mm lens at eye level, flat even fluorescent light with no direction, grey white and beige surfaces, tiled wall, glass partition, corridor slicing the figure into a narrow strip, one small red object, no close-up, no reaction shot, precise framing, cold, controlled, unresolved.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a middle-class family, a teacher, a doctor, an elderly couple; living room, kitchen, school corridor, hospital room, supermarket, an apartment viewed through a camcorder.
- 景别 / 构图：medium or wide only; the figure contained by walls, doors and corridors, cut into isolated blocks; precise symmetry or deliberate off-centering. Faces are never given a close-up to escape into.
- 镜头 / 焦段：35–50mm, close to human vision, no expressive wide angle; eye level, camera fixed at the far corner of the room, far enough to include the whole doorway.
- 光线：flat, even, directionless — fluorescent corridor light; no dramatic shadow, no window key.
- 色彩：grey, white, beige; red appears only after violence has already happened, while everyone pretends it is normal.
- 材质：tile, glass, white plaster, stainless steel, television screen, surveillance image, cheap office furniture.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动（只选一种）：completely static, locked-off surveillance frame — no push, no pan, no handheld.
- 主体运动：ordinary domestic action — washing dishes, tying shoes, walking down a corridor; during violence the camera stays on the empty room or cuts away.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：10–15 seconds, 24fps, no motion blur, duration held past comfort.

## 负面提示
no graphic on-screen violence, no thrilling impact shot, no reaction close-up, no sympathetic backstory, no score signalling sadness, no moving camera to escape, no fast-cut adrenaline, no noir shadows, no thematic speech, no cartoon bourgeois hypocrisy, no comforting resolution, no cold-graded arthouse cliché.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩 / 镜头 / 光线 / 构图 / 材质：grey, white, beige with a single red object; 35–50mm; flat fluorescent light; fixed wide frame from the room corner; tile, glass, monitor image.
- 禁止漂移：no teal grade, no shallow-focus portrait, no handheld intimacy, no dramatic backlight, no warm domestic glow, no drone or crane, no reaction shot.
