## 风格锚点
Cinematic epic fantasy film still, a tiny figure at the lower edge of a vast New Zealand mountain valley, layered foreground grass and distant peaks, saturated green and gold with grey-blue shadows, golden-hour god rays, aerial wide-angle perspective, deep landscape staging, 35mm grain, sweeping and earnest.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a small traveler on a ridge above a huge valley; a company crossing a stone bridge under a mountain; a lone figure before a towering dark gate.
- 景别 / 构图：extreme wide aerial landscape with the figure as a point, then a close-up on the face; deep diagonals and verticals, figure at the lower edge, landscape above; no flat postcard symmetry.
- 镜头 / 焦段：18–35mm wide to pull the landscape in; 50–85mm for faces; no telephoto compression of distance.
- 光线：golden hour, god rays through cloud, firelight, visible volumetric light; no flat interior lighting.
- 色彩：region-specific — saturated green and gold for home, grey-blue and black for the dark land, warm gold for the refuge; no single global grade.
- 材质：stone, moss, wool, leather, mud, metal, miniature-model texture; hands-on practical detail.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动：aerial crane descent, one continuous direction; no handheld, no whip pan.
- 主体运动：tiny figures walk across a ridge or through grass; banners and cloaks move in wind; no close acrobatics.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：6–8 seconds, 24fps, natural motion blur; keep the horizon and scale readable.

## 负面提示
no flat postcard symmetry, no single teal-orange grade, no pure CGI landscape, no handheld shake, no fast fragmented battle cutting, no cynical tone, no telephoto compression, no clean digital gloss, no drone tourism shot, no modern architecture.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩：region-specific saturated green-gold / grey-blue / warm gold, no global filter.
- 镜头：18–35mm wide, 50–85mm close, aerial perspective.
- 光线：golden hour, god rays, firelight, volumetric.
- 构图：deep diagonals and verticals, figure at the lower edge, landscape above.
- 材质：stone, moss, wool, leather, mud, miniature texture, 35mm grain.
- 禁止漂移：no modern glass towers, no neon, no digital skin, no single teal-orange grade.
