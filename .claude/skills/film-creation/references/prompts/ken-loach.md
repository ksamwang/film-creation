## 风格锚点
Social-realist film still, eye-level camera never above the subject, 35–50mm lens holding a medium or wide shot that keeps the whole room and body in frame, flat overcast British light through windows with no fill, grey, brown and faded green, no color grading, synthetic coats, worn carpet, plastic chairs, walls of official forms, unstaged, observational, quietly angry, no visual sympathy.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a carer, delivery driver or unemployed father at a job-centre counter; a family argument in a real living room; a union member waiting in a corridor; the room and the person share the frame.
- 景别 / 构图：medium or wide that holds both body and room; preserve economic information — how big the room is, what is on the table, who stands in the doorway; no decorative foreground, no poverty-as-silhouette.
- 镜头 / 焦段：35–50mm, medium shot at a distance that still shows the whole body; no long lens stealing emotion.
- 光线：natural window light and practical room lamps, minimal fill; one side of the face may fall dark; never relit into even studio light.
- 色彩：grey, brown, faded green, close to an overcast British day; no warm grade, no saturation lift.
- 材质：synthetic coats, worn carpet, plastic chairs, walls covered with official forms and notices.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动（只选一种）：light handheld follow — the camera walks behind a person through a corridor, through a door and down into a chair; the shake comes from footsteps, never from emotion.
- 主体运动：queueing, sitting through an assessment, arguing inside one continuous medium shot, walking with fatigue; no staged outburst.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：8–12 seconds, 24fps, natural motion blur only, no slow motion.

## 负面提示
no single villain, no evil official, no outsider savior (no lawyer, journalist or middle-class rescuer), no score telling the audience to be angry, no squalor close-ups, no full social-analysis speech, no uplifting ending, no "he succeeded later" epilogue, no cutting a meeting into reaction shots, no actors playing pathetic, no shaky-cam-as-realism, no grey filter cliché.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩：grey / brown / faded green, ungraded, low saturation.
- 镜头：35–50mm, medium and wide.
- 光线：natural window and practical light, minimal fill, one side of the face dark.
- 构图：eye level never above the subject, person and room together, continuous readable geography.
- 材质：synthetic clothing, worn carpet, plastic furniture, paper forms.
- 禁止漂移：no handheld melodrama, no teal-orange grade, no shallow-focus beauty, no modern glass offices, no drone shots, no digital skin.
