## 风格锚点
Anamorphic 35mm film still, deep-focus widescreen staging, blue moonlight through windows, warm tungsten practicals, hard shadows, foreground framing, a suburban street or corridor receding into darkness, 35–50mm Panavision, visible grain, 1980s optical texture, patient, quietly mythic, everyday space made ominous.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a figure in a suburban house, hospital corridor, garage, or night street; the room and its far corners stay visible.
- 景别 / 构图：wide to medium-wide; deep focus, foreground doorframe or furniture, figure in midground, background light source.
- 镜头 / 焦段：35–50mm anamorphic; 25–35mm to establish the whole space.
- 光线：blue moonlight through windows plus warm tungsten practicals; ratio –; hard shadows, visible source.
- 色彩：blue-cyan against amber, low saturation, deep blacks, no neon.
- 材质：fog, rain, wet glass, linoleum, wood paneling, brushed metal, film grain.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动（只选一种）：slow Steadicam push-in.
- 主体运动：the figure walks away down the corridor or toward a lit doorway; the camera follows at a patient distance.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：10–20 seconds, 24fps, natural motion blur, no slow motion.

## 负面提示
no shallow depth of field, no blurred background, no even fill light, no blue filter wash, no modern LED white, no fast cutting, no neon collage, no empty foreground, no digital cleanliness, no handheld shake.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩 / 镜头 / 光线 / 构图 / 材质：blue-cyan and amber, low saturation, deep black; 35–50mm anamorphic; moonlight plus practicals at –; deep-focus foreground frame; fog, wet glass, grain.
- 禁止漂移：no shallow focus, no LED white, no neon, no clean digital look, no blue monochrome grade.
