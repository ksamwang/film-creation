## 风格锚点
Cinematic film still, a West African village courtyard at noon, a woman in a white headwrap facing the camera in frontal medium shot, bystanders planted on both sides as witnesses, hard unmodified midday sun, mud-brick wall, indigo cloth, corrugated iron, 35mm standard lens, static camera, symmetrical staging, plain unfiltered color, public, declarative, no one is decorative.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a woman facing the camera in a market or village courtyard, a group of neighbours standing as witnesses; a polygamous household gathered outside; a man addressing a small public from the front, not from a podium.
- 景别 / 构图：frontal medium shot for the speaker; medium-wide for the group; stable, symmetrical, plainly composed; inside/outside the doorway is the power diagram; clarity over beauty.
- 镜头 / 焦段：35–50mm standard, near eye level; little telephoto; deep enough focus that witnesses stay readable.
- 光线：natural light; hard midday sun accepted; no diffusion, no cinematic rim light.
- 色彩：earth tones, white cloth, indigo blue; ungraded; no filter, no sepia.
- 材质：rough cloth, mud-brick wall, corrugated iron, plastic buckets, enamelware.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动（只选一种）：static camera at eye level, framed like a public hearing; no push-in, no handheld.
- 主体运动：a person speaks directly toward the lens while bystanders listen, shift, interrupt; someone enters through the doorway and the group re-forms; no private gesture.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：8–12 seconds, 24fps, minimal motion blur, no slow motion.

## 负面提示
no exotic sepia or folkloric score, no unnamed generic Africa, no charming colonial villain, no internal monologue replacing public debate, no private reconciled ending, no agitated camera, no two-person-only confrontation, no women as pure victims, no picturesque poverty, no glamorous close-up lighting.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩 / 镜头 / 光线 / 构图 / 材质：earth, white, indigo / 35–50mm, / hard natural sun, no fill / frontal medium, symmetrical, door inside/outside / rough cloth, mud wall, iron, enamel.
- 禁止漂移：no sepia, no drone, no modern city glass, no handheld shake, no shallow-focus beauty portrait, no clean digital skin.
