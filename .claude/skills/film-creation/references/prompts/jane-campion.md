## 风格锚点
Cinematic film still, a rain-soaked colonial forest, a woman's hand and mud-caked boot in the foreground, 85mm lens close to the skin, damp grey-blue overcast light, wet wool and lace, branches obscuring half her body at the frame edge, soft film grain, desire held in the body, language arriving too late.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a mute woman and her daughter on a grey-blue beach with a piano crate; a man's hand resting on wool; a woman sewing by a window, her face partly behind a curtain; a figure isolated in a wet forest.
- 景别 / 构图：close-up on skin first — hand, lips, nape, feet in mud, skin under cloth; wide only to dwarf or isolate; figure pressed into a corner, foreground branch, sheet or curtain covering half the body.
- 镜头 / 焦段：50–85mm close, macro for hands and fabric; 24–35mm for the landscape that judges; eye level with the body.
- 光线：natural — window, candle, sea reflection, overcast; soft but directional; no glamour key.
- 色彩：one film one code — grey-blue beach and muddy brown forest, or dry yellow and black; muted, damp, no clean primaries.
- 材质：mud, rain, wool, lace, leather, wet skin.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动（只选一种）：slow push-in toward a hand or the back of a neck, then hold; no handheld.
- 主体运动：hands sew, write, chop wood, touch fabric; a body breathes; someone turns away rather than speaking; no theatrical gesture.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：5–8 seconds, 24fps, soft natural blur, no slow-motion emphasis.

## 负面提示
no score announcing passion, no flawless nude spectacle, no modern-language self-explanation by the woman, no colonial conscience ending, no postcard landscape wide, no pure villain male, no fast cutting in intimacy, no complete embrace ending, no candlelit soft-focus romance, no glossy skin without sweat, mud or cloth marks.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩 / 镜头 / 光线 / 构图 / 材质：grey-blue and mud brown, or dry yellow and black / 50–85mm close, body / natural window, candle, sea reflection / eye-level close, figure in corner, foreground occlusion / mud, rain, wool, lace, leather.
- 禁止漂移：no drone landscape, no HDR sea, no clean modern clothing, no digital beauty skin, no warm golden filter.
