> 用法：「风格锚点」可直接放在模式 A 提示词末尾当风格句；「负面提示」和句中的 no ... 只在模型有 negative 参数时使用（模式 B 放进 NEGATIVE），否则改写成正面描述；「视频提示」的镜头运动按图生视频写，文生视频要先补人物位置。见 `../prompting.md`。

## 风格锚点
Classic Hollywood studio portrait, black and white, 35mm, high-key butterfly key through silk, soft fill from camera left, hair light and shoulder rim, face at to with no hard shadow, background one to two stops down, gauze diffusion, satin and fur, full tonal range from velvet black to satin white, centered symmetrical composition, glamorous, composed, immaculate.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a star in a studio set — a drawing room, a dressing room, a staircase; painted backdrop, polished furniture, nothing out of place.
- 景别 / 构图：medium close-up or close-up, centered and symmetrical, the face at the center or on the thirds, background subdued.
- 镜头 / 焦段：50mm or 75–100mm portrait lens, slight compression, soft-focus portrait glass.
- 光线：butterfly key through silk, frontal fill, hair and shoulder rim, – on the face, background 1–2 stops down.
- 色彩：black and white with full range; or Metrocolor pastels — cream, pale pink, mint green, powder blue.
- 材质：satin, fur, crystal, polished wood, chiffon, painted canvas.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动（只选一种）：slow dolly push-in to the close-up on the emotional beat.
- 主体运动：the star turns her head, lifts a cigarette, walks into the light and stops.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：6–12 seconds, 24fps, minimal motion blur, no handheld, no speed ramp.

## 负面提示
no hard shadow on the face, no low-key practical lighting, no handheld, no cool blue or teal-orange grade, no background brighter than the face, no wide-angle face, no fake film grain, no dirty costumes or sets, no naturalism, no modern glass.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩 / 镜头 / 光线 / 构图 / 材质：full-range black and white or pastel Metrocolor; 50–100mm portrait lens; butterfly key plus hair rim at –; centered symmetry; satin, fur and polished wood.
- 禁止漂移：no modern cool grade, no handheld, no hard side light, no grime, no digital skin smoothing.
