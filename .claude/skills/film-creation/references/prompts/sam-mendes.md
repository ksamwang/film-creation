## 风格锚点
Cinematic wide film still, classical symmetrical composition, a figure framed by a doorway or trench wall, muted olive-grey and brown palette with one warm amber interior source, soft directional window light, deep staged space, foreground obstruction, 35mm film grain, restrained and theatrical, eye-level.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a soldier moving through a mud trench; a man standing alone in a doorway of a suburban house; two figures separated by a long dining table.
- 景别 / 构图：medium or wide first, letting the body occupy the room; then a close-up framed by a door or window; classical symmetry, deep staging, figures on architectural axes; no fragmented coverage.
- 镜头 / 焦段：35–50mm for readable space; 75–100mm for close-ups; no wide-angle distortion, no telephoto voyeurism.
- 光线：window light and practicals, soft but directional, figures often backlit into silhouette; no even commercial lighting.
- 色彩：muted olive, grey, brown; one warm amber source per interior; no saturated primaries.
- 材质：wood, glass, mud, water, wool, smoke; period detail, 35mm grain.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动：steady Steadicam follow, one continuous direction; no handheld shake, no cutaway.
- 主体运动：the figure walks through a trench or corridor, passes foreground cover, keeps moving; no theatrical gesture.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：6–10 seconds, 24fps, natural motion blur; keep the horizon level.

## 负面提示
no handheld shake, no fast cutting, no dutch angle, no saturated color, no music-video slow motion, no shaky war chaos, no clean digital sharpness, no centered hero pose, no glossy period drama, no obvious steadicam showmanship.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩：olive-grey / brown / one amber interior source, low saturation.
- 镜头：35–50mm main lens, 75–100mm close-up, or .
- 光线：window and practical light, soft directional, backlit silhouette.
- 构图：classical symmetry, doorway or window frame, deep staged space.
- 材质：wood, glass, mud, water, wool, smoke, 35mm grain.
- 禁止漂移：no modern glass architecture, no neon, no digital skin, no drone aerial.
