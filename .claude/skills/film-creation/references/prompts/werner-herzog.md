## 风格锚点
Cinematic film still, ecstatic documentary, extreme wide landscape, a human figure reduced to a speck at the lower edge, sky or mountain filling two-thirds of the frame, low camera near water level, 25mm wide lens, natural light with backlit fog and rain, the figure half-swallowed by glare, deep black-green jungle and blinding white ice, mud, river, rusted boat hull, 16mm grain, doomed, obsessive, awe-struck.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a conquistador, a boat dragged over a mountain, a feral foundling, a man walking a straight line through a market; river, desert, glacier, fog forest, ruins — terrain that obstructs.
- 景别 / 构图：extreme wide first, the human a stain on the landscape, pressed to the lower edge, sky or mountain at two-thirds; to see the will, cut straight to a face — no medium transition.
- 镜头 / 焦段：25–35mm wide that swallows the figure; 100–200mm long lens only to compress the mountain against the body.
- 光线：natural light only — backlight, fog, rain, snow; the figure is eaten by glare, half in silhouette.
- 色彩：natural ungraded color, green that goes black, white that burns; no filter warmth, no sunset romance.
- 材质：mud, river water, rusted hull, ice, wet canvas, dust, ruins.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动（只选一种）：static contemplative hold on the landscape, or heavy handheld follow behind the figure — never both in one shot.
- 主体运动：slow, laborious progress against terrain; dragging, wading, climbing; the body consumes itself while the landscape does not move.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：8–12 seconds, 24fps, slight natural blur on handheld, movement heavy and friction-bound.

## 负面提示
no hysterical madness, no picturesque travel-brochure landscape, no postcard composition, no psychological backstory, no sentimental score, no freak-show framing of non-actors, no clean studio lighting, no closed causal narrative, no redemptive ending, no drone-smooth aerial beauty, no warm vacation grade, no medium-shot coverage.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩 / 镜头 / 光线 / 构图 / 材质：black-green and blinding white, ungraded; 25–35mm wide; natural backlight and fog; figure at lower edge, sky at two-thirds; mud, water, rust, ice.
- 禁止漂移：no golden-hour postcard, no saturated HDR, no clean CGI vista, no smooth gimbal glide, no modern city, no heroic low-angle portrait, no clear resolution.
