> 用法：「风格锚点」可直接放在模式 A 提示词末尾当风格句；「负面提示」和句中的 no ... 只在模型有 negative 参数时使用（模式 B 放进 NEGATIVE），否则改写成正面描述；「视频提示」的镜头运动按图生视频写，文生视频要先补人物位置。见 `../prompting.md`。

## 风格锚点
Soft-focus romantic film still, large diffused source wrapping the face, gentle halation around practicals, amber and cream skin against a cool grey-blue background, low contrast with detail held in the shadows, 40–50mm lens at eye level, medium close-up, gauze and smoke in the air, 35mm grain, intimate, warm, unhurried.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a person in an interior lit by practical lamps and a large off-screen soft source; curtains, smoke, polished wood.
- 景别 / 构图：medium close-up to medium shot, subject slightly off-centre, background soft but layered.
- 镜头 / 焦段：40–50mm for the body, 75–85mm for close-ups; shallow but not creamy depth of field.
- 光线：large diffusion or bounced source from 45 degrees above, to ratio, highlight blooming softly.
- 色彩：amber, cream, warm skin, cool grey-blue background; low saturation, no primary colour shocks.
- 材质：gauze, smoke, velvet, soft fabric, glowing practical lamps, polished wood.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动（只选一种）：slow dolly in.
- 主体运动：the subject turns toward the light or settles into a chair; small gestures only.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：6–10 seconds, 24fps, natural motion blur, no slow motion.

## 负面提示
no hard unfiltered key light, no sharp shadow edges, no handheld shake, no cold white skin, no high-saturation primaries, no background sharper than the face, no crushed black shadows, no misty out-of-focus haze, no modern glass-and-steel interior.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩 / 镜头 / 光线 / 构图 / 材质：amber-cream skin, cool background; 40–50mm; large diffused source; eye-level medium close-up; gauze and smoke.
- 禁止漂移：no neutral digital white, no hard sun through windows, no teal-orange grade, no wedding-soft cliché, no modern LED.
