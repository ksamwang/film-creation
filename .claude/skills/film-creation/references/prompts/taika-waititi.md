> 用法：「风格锚点」可直接放在模式 A 提示词末尾当风格句；「负面提示」和句中的 no ... 只在模型有 negative 参数时使用（模式 B 放进 NEGATIVE），否则改写成正面描述；「视频提示」的镜头运动按图生视频写，文生视频要先补人物位置。见 `../prompting.md`。

## 风格锚点
New Zealand deadpan comedy, flat overcast daylight, centered family-photo composition, 40mm lens at eye level, muted fern green, sky blue and ochre, wool sweaters, old cars, grass and wooden houses, a whole ensemble standing together, nobody glamorous, fine grain, dry, warm, anti-heroic.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a scruffy man and a child in matching wool sweaters standing in tall fern, a small-town living room with mismatched furniture, a school hall.
- 景别 / 构图：full-body group shot, centered and nearly symmetrical, everyone facing front; someone looks into the lens; no artful off-center.
- 镜头 / 焦段：35–50mm at eye level; 70–200mm compression only for a reaction cut-in.
- 光线：flat overcast daylight or a single household bulb; soft, no direction; no rim light.
- 色彩：muted fern green, sky blue, ochre, faded knitwear; low contrast, natural saturation; no teal-orange.
- 材质：wool, old car paint, grass, weathered wood, plastic toys, a sudden cartoonish patch of blood.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动：static camera, then one snap zoom to the face and hold; no other movement.
- 主体运动：someone delivers a deadpan line without moving; a child beside him reacts with a tiny shrug; the group stays still.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：4–6 seconds, 24fps, minimal motion blur.

## 负面提示
no music cue under the joke, no cut immediately after the punchline, no colonial gaze, no noble-savage imagery, no terrifying authority figure, no saturated warm nostalgia filter, no handheld shake as comedy, no character explaining trauma, no heroic victory ending, no glamorous lighting.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩：muted fern green, sky blue, ochre, faded knitwear, low contrast.
- 镜头：35–50mm eye level; snap zoom only.
- 光线：flat overcast daylight or one household bulb, no rim.
- 构图：centered group, family-photo frontality, direct address allowed.
- 材质：wool, old car paint, grass, weathered wood, plastic.
- 禁止漂移：no teal-orange grade, no drone landscape, no glamour retouch, no colonial costume drama, no digital skin.
